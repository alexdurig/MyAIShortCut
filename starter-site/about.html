About

A small firm on purpose, so you always know who's doing your books.

Built by an owner, for owners

I spent years watching good businesses fly blind on their own finances — not because the owners didn't care, but because bookkeeping is tedious and nobody taught them how.

Bluebird exists so a ten-person company has the same financial clarity as a ten-million-dollar one. We keep the books clean, explain them plainly, and leave the decisions to you.

How we work

Fully remote, entirely cloud-based. We meet by video, communicate by email, and keep a shared folder of your documents so nothing lives only in someone's inbox.
